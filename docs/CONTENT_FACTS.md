- **Prof. Folasade Ogunsola**: Vice-Chancellor, University of Lagos

### Statistics (Verified)
- **8 Universities**: In Nigeria Cohort 1 (UNILAG, UI, OAU, UNIBEN, ABU, UNN, FUTA, UNIPORT)
- **500,000+ Students**: Target for digital skills training across Nigeria
- **13 African Countries**: With UniPods under Timbuktoo Initiative
- **2,000+ Startups**: Targeted university-linked startups

### Timbuktoo Initiative Facts
- **Launch**: 2023 by UNDP Africa
- **Goal**: Create 10 million digital jobs by 2030
- **Investment**: $1 billion commitment
- **Hubs**: 10 thematic innovation hubs across Africa
- **Countries**: 13 African nations participating
