What is the AI UniPod?

An Innovation Hub
Built for the Future

The AI UniPod is a state-of-the-art University Innovation Pod at the University of Lagos. It's the first AI-themed UniPod in Nigeria, and one of 13 UniPods established across Africa by UNDP's Timbuktoo Initiative.

A joint initiative of UNILAG and UNDP Nigeria, the facility features an AI Lab, Maker Space, Design Studio, Pitch Garage, and collaborative workspaces — all flowing together in a space infused with African heritage, plants, and natural light.

Inside the Pod

State-of-the-Art.
Designed for Africa.

Six dedicated spaces from the AI Lab to the Pitch Garage, all flowing into one another, infused with plants, African artworks, and the spirit of communal innovation.

AI Lab

High-performance computing workstations, robotics components, and machine learning tools.

Maker Space

3D printers, resin printers, and CO₂ laser machine for rapid prototyping.

Design Studio

Creative workspace for UX/UI, product design, and digital fabrication.

Pitch Garage

Professional pitch space connecting innovators with investors.
