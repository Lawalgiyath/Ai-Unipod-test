The AI UniPod

A sovereignty conversation.
Will Africa own the algorithms
that govern its future?

Situated at the heart of the University of Lagos, the AI UniPod is not just an innovation lab, it is Africa's declaration that the continent will build its own AI, on its own terms, for its own people.

"Artificial intelligence will determine who thrives and who is left behind in the 21st century. For Africa, it is not just a technology conversation. It is a sovereignty conversation."

Ms. Ahunna Eziakonwa — UN Assistant Secretary-General, UNDP Regional Director for Africa

The Story

From Groundbreaking
to Groundbreaking

  1. April 2024

    UNILAG hosts UNDP's Resident Representative Elsie Attafuah. The decision is made: Lagos will be home to Nigeria's first AI-themed University Innovation Pod.

  2. July 2024

    Official groundbreaking ceremony on Commercial Road, Akoka Campus. Ms. Ahunna Eziakonwa delivers a landmark lecture: Nigeria: The Anchor of Africa's Economic Transformation.

  3. March 2025

    UNILAG and UNDP sign a landmark MoU. PODEX Associates presents designs for an AI Lab, Maker Space, Pitch Garage, and Design Studio — flowing together in a space infused with African heritage.

  4. September 2025

    UNILAG joins seven other universities at UNDP's national workshop in Ikoyi to finalize the UniPod Activation Roadmap — marking the shift from establishment to implementation.

  5. March 2026

    Open House Day. Students, faculty, and UNDP tour the completed facility. The AI UniPod opens — equipped with 3D printers, CO₂ laser machines, robotics components, and more.

Inside the Pod

State-of-the-Art.
Designed for Africa.

Six dedicated spaces
from the AI Lab to the Pitch Garage, all flowing into one another, infused with plants, African artworks, and the spirit of communal innovation.

AI Lab

High-performance computing workstations, robotics components, and machine learning tools for deep experimentation.

Maker Space

PCD printers, resin and 3D printers, and a CO₂ laser machine for rapid physical prototyping.

Design Lab & Studio

A creative workspace for UX/UI, product design, visual storytelling, and digital fabrication.

Pitch Garage

A professional pitch and presentation space connecting innovators with investors and ecosystem partners.

Collaboration Areas

Open co-working zones and enclosed glass pods for team ideation, mentorship, and workshops.

Exhibition Space

A curated gallery and event space for showcasing innovations, hosting panels, and welcoming partners.
