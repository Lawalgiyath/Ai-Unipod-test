Dr. Victor Odumuyiwa

Director, Nithub UNILAG / UniPod Focal Point

Dr. Victor Odumuyiwa holds a PhD from Lorraine University, France, and is an MIT ETT Fellow. He serves as the Director of the NITDA IT Hub (Nithub) at the University of Lagos.

As the Principal Investigator for the EduAIHub project and the operational lead of the UNILAG AI UniPod, Dr. Odumuyiwa is a relentless champion of AI education and practical innovation in Nigeria.

He oversees the day-to-day execution of the UniPod's programs, ensuring that students and researchers have the tools, mentorship, and environment they need to succeed.
