About the AI UniPod

Nigeria's First.
Africa's Catalyst.

The UNILAG AI UniPod is where Africa's next generation of innovators, engineers, and entrepreneurs build the AI-powered solutions that will define the continent's future.

What is the AI UniPod?

An Innovation Hub
Built for the Future

The AI UniPod is a state-of-the-art University Innovation Pod situated at the University of Lagos, Commercial Road, Akoka Campus. It is the first AI-themed UniPod in Nigeria, and one of 13 UniPods established across Africa by UNDP's Timbuktoo Initiative.

The facility is a joint initiative of the University of Lagos (UNILAG) and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) Nigeria, grounded in a Memorandum of Understanding signed in March 2025. It is operated through UNILAG's NITDA Information Technology Development Agency hub (Nithub).

Architecturally designed by PODEX Associates Limited, the pod's spaces artistically flow into each other — embodying the communal essence of African heritage while maintaining the functionality of a dynamic collaborative hub. Plants, African artworks, and natural light create a calming, welcoming, and inspiring environment.

Journey

How We Got Here

April 2024
The First Conversation

Vice-Chancellor Prof. Folasade Ogunsola hosts UNDP Resident Representative Elsie Attafuah at UNILAG. UNILAG expresses readiness to host the first AI UniPod in Nigeria.

July 10, 2024
Groundbreaking Ceremony

Official groundbreaking on Commercial Road, Akoka Campus. UNDP's Ms. Ahunna Eziakonwa delivers the public lecture: "Nigeria: The Anchor of Africa's Economic Transformation." The pod is expected by December 2024.

October 2024
Construction Contract Awarded

PODEX Associates Limited, the architectural firm bringing the UniPod to life, is engaged. UNDP issues a procurement notice for remodelling, repurposing, and furnishing the space.

March 18, 2025
MOU Signed

UNILAG and UNDP officially sign the Memorandum of Understanding, cementing their partnership. PODEX presents final designs featuring an AI Lab, Design Studio, Pitch Garage, Maker Space, and Collaboration Areas — delivery date: June 2025.

September 2025
National Activation Workshop

UNILAG joins 7 other universities at UNDP's UniPod Activation Workshop in Ikoyi, Lagos. A comprehensive roadmap, SOPs, and visibility framework are finalized for Nigeria's Cohort 1 UniPods.

December 2025
TETFund–UNDP Partnership

TETFund and UNDP sign a landmark national MoU to extend UniPod infrastructure to universities and polytechnics nationwide — targeting 500,000+ students and 2,000 startups.

March 10–12, 2026
Open House Days

The completed AI UniPod opens its doors to UNILAG's academic community. Ahead of its official launch, students, staff, and UNDP tour the state-of-the-art facility.

2026 — Official Launch
The Story Continues…

The AI UniPod officially launches. The first innovation cohort is onboarded. Africa's AI future begins here.
