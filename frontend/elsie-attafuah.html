Elsie G. Attafuah

Resident Representative, UNDP Nigeria

Ms. Elsie G. Attafuah is the Resident Representative for the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in Nigeria.

She has been the driving force behind UNDP Nigeria's partnership with UNILAG, officially signing the Memorandum of Understanding and co-leading the national UniPod activation strategy alongside the Nigerian government.

Her leadership ensures that the Timbuktoo initiative reaches the brightest young minds in Nigeria, empowering them to build homegrown solutions to local and global challenges.
