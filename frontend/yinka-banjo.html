Prof. Chika Yinka-Banjo

Director, AI UniPod Research

Professor Chika Yinka-Banjo is a Professor of Computer Science and a leading AI and Robotics researcher in Nigeria.

As the Director of AI UniPod Research, she leads the academic and investigative agenda at the Pod. Her work drives innovation in AI education, ensuring that the research conducted within the UniPod meets global standards while addressing uniquely African challenges.

She mentors the next generation of data scientists, machine learning engineers, and robotics experts building out of the UniPod.
